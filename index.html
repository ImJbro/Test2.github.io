Overview

Prometheus Flame is a live-fire, multi-phase competition to dismantle closed, proprietary pipelines and replace them with an open, modular ecosystem for Autonomy and Mission Systems. Teams iterate fast, publish openly, and prove capability in real fly-off events—turning ideas into deployable, standards-compliant solutions at the speed of software.

“Create an open-source, enthusiast and dual-use-grade learning platform that establishes a vibrant community around standards-compliant Autonomy and Mission Systems product lines.”
— Prometheus Flame Draft Request for Solutions

Problem

The system we rely on today was not built to move at the speed of modern conflict.

Every modification—no matter how small—triggers costly re-certification cycles, forcing programs to choose between speed and safety. Integration becomes negotiation. Innovation becomes risk. And the pace of delivery falls behind the pace of the threat.

The result is a system that cannot scale, cannot adapt, and cannot win in a contested, rapidly evolving environment.

Without a shift to open standards, modular design, and a competitive ecosystem, the Department will continue to invest in systems that are obsolete the moment they are fielded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can teams enter after Phase I?
Yes. The challenge is structured as an open competition. Offerors may enter at any time, and participation in a prior phase is not required.
What will be evaluated?
Evaluation focuses on mission performance, usability and onboarding, technical implementation and standards compliance, and risk.
What are early-phase deliverables?
Competitive early-phase solutions should include open-source implementations, turn-key example projects, public repositories or websites, getting-started guides, documentation, and instructional videos.
